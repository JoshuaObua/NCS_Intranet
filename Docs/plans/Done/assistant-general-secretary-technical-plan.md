## 1. Executive Role Overview & Technical Command Architecture

The **Assistant General Secretary - Technical (AGS-T)** is the executive head charged with overseeing and harmonizing the entire technical ecosystem of the National Council of Sports (NCS). The AGS-T bridges high-performance sports policy, federation governance, athlete talent pathways, and infrastructure engineering to ensure Uganda's sports sector operates at world-class standards.

The AGS-T functions as the **First-Line Executive Vetting and Technical Clearance Authority** before items escalate to the General Secretary for statutory Accounting Officer sign-off.

## 2. Key Executive Modules & Functional Capabilities

---

### 2.1 Technical & Sports Administration Oversight Engine
1. **Federation 360° Monitor (`50+ Registered Associations`):**
   - Real-time compliance rating per federation (FUFA, UAF, UNF, UBF, Uganda Rugby, etc.).
   - Annual General Meeting (AGM) compliance, constitution alignment under National Sports Act 2023, executive term expirations, and election monitoring.
   - Activity calendar tracking: National championships, leagues, and grassroots tournaments.
2. **National Sports Management Information System (NSMIS) Live Stream:**
   - Central registry of 15,000+ national athletes, 1,200+ certified coaches, and technical officials.
   - National ranking leaderboards, age-category verifications, and biometric passport logs.
3. **Federation Funding & Grant Technical Recommendation:**
   - Evaluates quarterly federation funding requests against approved activity plans and past performance metrics.
   - Signs off on Technical Recommendations before submittal to Finance & GS for payment release.
4. **International Games Delegations & National Teams Hub:**
   - Pre-clearance of Team Uganda national delegations (Olympics, Commonwealth Games, All Africa Games, World Championships).
   - Athlete entry lists, technical official credentials, visa clearance letters, and international travel logistics.
5. **Safeguarding & Anti-Doping Monitoring:**
   - Oversees compliance with World Anti-Doping Code (WADA/RADO), athlete Therapeutic Use Exemptions (TUE), and IOC Safeguarding protocols.

---

### 2.2 Engineering, Infrastructure & Facility Health Oversight
1. **Facility Health Matrix & Real-Time Monitoring:**
   - **Lugogo Sports Complex:** National Indoor Arena, Cricket Oval, Tennis Courts, Hockey Grounds, NCS Hostel Blocks, Administration Headquarters.
   - **Regional Sports Hubs & Stadia:** Inspection reports, land encroachment alerts, and upgrade feasibility studies for regional sports facilities.
2. **Engineering Work Order Review Pipeline:**
   - Real-time visibility into all active work orders across Civil and Electrical teams (Senior Engineer, Assistant Engineers, Plumber, Electrician).
   - Priority status flags: `EMERGENCY_CRITICAL`, `MAJOR_CAPEX`, `PREVENTATIVE_MAINTENANCE`, `ROUTINE`.
3. **CapEx & Major Works Technical Endorsement:**
   - Technical vetting of Bill of Quantities (BOQ), architectural drawings, and contractor bids for civil works before PPDA procurement and GS sign-off.
4. **Facility Match-Day & Tournament Readiness Certification:**
   - Electronic inspection checklist covering floodlight lux levels, turf condition, pitch marking, scoreboard operation, public address systems, emergency exits, and medical room readiness.
   - Issuance of formal **NCS Facility Readiness Certificate** for high-profile national and international fixtures.

---

### 2.3 Sports Asset, Gear & Fixed Asset Register Oversight
1. **Complete Fixed Asset & Gear Inventory (`UGX 32,175,914,535.00` Scope):**
   - 100% Data parity with `Docs/FIXED ASSET REGISTER ADJUSTMENTS.xlsx` (297 assets across Land, Buildings, Vehicles, ICT, Electrical Machinery, Furniture).
   - Executive monitoring of asset valuation, revaluations (`FB_COST` to `ADJUSTED COST`), and IPSAS 17 accumulated depreciation & Net Book Value.
2. **Federation Equipment Pool & Loan Register:**
   - Tracks high-performance equipment loaned to federations for international qualifiers or national championships with return condition inspections.
3. **Maintenance Spare Parts & Consumables Inspection:**
   - Oversees plumbing, electrical, and turf maintenance inventory to prevent facility downtime.

---

### 2.4 Executive Technical Decision & Endorsement Queue
A unified workflow queue where AGS-T acts upon items submitted from Technical and Engineering departments:
- **Queue Actions:** `Endorse & Escalate to GS`, `Approve (Delegated Authority)`, `Return for Technical Rectification`, `Reject with Minute`.
- **Items Processed:**
  - Federation Grant Technical Clearance.
  - Engineering Work Orders > UGX 2,000,000.
  - National Team Travel & Delegation Cleared Lists.
  - Facility Booking Technical Feasibility Sign-offs.
  - Technical Procurement Form 5 Endorsements.

## 4. Database Schema & Technical Architecture

```sql
-- Technical approval endorsements by AGS-T
CREATE TABLE technical_approvals (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    reference_no VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    category VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- FEDERATION_GRANT, INFRASTRUCTURE_CAPEX, DELEGATION_CLEARANCE, VENUE_CERTIFICATION
    originating_department VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- TECHNICAL_SPORTS, ENGINEERING, FACILITIES
    submitted_by UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id),
    title VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    description TEXT,
    financial_implication_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    supporting_documents JSONB DEFAULT '[]'::jsonb,
    agst_status VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'PENDING_REVIEW', -- PENDING_REVIEW, ENDORSED_TO_GS, APPROVED, RETURNED_FOR_CORRECTION, REJECTED
    agst_reviewed_by UUID REFERENCES users(id),
    agst_reviewed_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE,
    agst_remarks TEXT,
    escalated_to_gs BOOLEAN DEFAULT FALSE,
    gs_statutory_status VARCHAR(32) DEFAULT 'PENDING_GS', -- PENDING_GS, APPROVED_BY_GS, REJECTED_BY_GS
    gs_approved_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
    updated_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- Facility readiness certifications issued by AGS-T
CREATE TABLE facility_readiness_certifications (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    certificate_no VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL,
    facility_name VARCHAR(128) NOT NULL, -- Lugogo Indoor Arena, Lugogo Stadium, Tennis Complex
    event_name VARCHAR(255) NOT NULL,
    inspecting_engineer_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id),
    structural_integrity_passed BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    lighting_lux_level INT NOT NULL, -- Minimum 1200 lux for broadcast
    turf_court_score NUMERIC(4,1) NOT NULL, -- e.g. 9.5 / 10
    sanitation_water_ok BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    emergency_safety_ok BOOLEAN DEFAULT TRUE,
    readiness_rating NUMERIC(5,2) NOT NULL, -- e.g. 96.5%
    agst_signature_id UUID REFERENCES users(id),
    certification_status VARCHAR(32) DEFAULT 'CERTIFIED_READY', -- CERTIFIED_READY, CONDITIONAL, REJECTED
    certified_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);
```
