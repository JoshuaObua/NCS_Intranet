## 1. Executive Master Appraisal & Valuation Architecture

Under statutory governance rules in Uganda, the **General Secretary (GS)** is personally accountable as the Accounting Officer for the condition, valuation, and efficiency of all state assets entrusted to the National Council of Sports, as well as the performance of human capital and the fiscal discipline of public funds.

The **GS Master Appraisal & Valuation Center** is an executive analytical dashboard integrated directly into `NCS_Intranet`. It consolidates data from Accounting, Internal Audit, Engineering, Stores, HR, and Sports Administration into a single high-level evaluation suite.

## 2. Deep-Dive Executive Appraisal Pillars

---

### 2.1 Pillar I: Property & Inventory Appraisal (Facilities & Stores)

#### A. Real Property & Sports Infrastructure Appraisal
1. **Physical Property Registry & Valuation:**
   - Evaluates all NCS-titled real estate: Plots 2-10 Coronation Avenue Lugogo, Lugogo National Indoor Arena, NCS Head Office Complex, Lugogo Hostels Block, Tennis Club Pavilions, Cricket Oval, Hockey Stadium, and Regional properties.
2. **Structural & Engineering Health Index:**
   - Real-time engineering condition ratings fed directly from the Engineering Department (Civil, Electrical, Plumbing).
   - Structural safety score, roof integrity, drainage capacity, and floodlight operational status.
3. **Property Commercial Yield & Utilization:**
   - Rental yield comparison, occupancy percentage of hostels, arena booking utilization hours, and commercial revenue generated vs maintenance costs.
4. **Land Boundary & Title Integrity Tracker:**
   - Cadastral survey status, acreage verification, ground lease renewals, and land encroachment risk monitoring.

#### B. Stores, Supplies & Sports Equipment Inventory Appraisal
1. **Sports Equipment & Match Gear Valuation:**
   - Current replacement value and physical condition grading of national competition apparatus (synthetic tracks, gym equipment, boxing rings, timing systems, balls, nets).
2. **Stock Movement, Turnover & Aging:**
   - Fast-moving vs slow-moving stock ratios in NCS central warehouses.
   - Consumables burn rate (cleaning chemicals, turf fertilizers, electrical spares).
3. **Obsolescence & Shrinkage Appraisal:**
   - Automated detection of damaged, expired, or obsolete stock items.
   - Reconciliation of physical stock counts against book records.

---

### 2.2 Pillar II: Performance Appraisal (Staff, Departmental & Financial Performance)

#### A. Staff 360° Performance Appraisal Evaluation
1. **Employee Performance Scorecards (`128 Active Staff`):**
   - Individual annual and semi-annual appraisal ratings (Scale 1-5: Unsatisfactory, Needs Improvement, Meets Expectations, Exceeds Expectations, Outstanding).
   - Balanced Scorecard (BSC) breakdown: Operational Goals, Core Competencies, Innovation, Integrity & Public Service Values.
2. **Performance Bell-Curve & Reward Queue:**
   - Distribution matrix of staff scores across departments.
   - Executive approval queue for merit increments, contract renewals, promotions, and Performance Improvement Plans (PIP).

#### B. Departmental Operational Delivery Index
- Evaluation of delivery rates against annual operational plans across all 8 departments (Technical, Engineering, HR, Finance, PDU, PR, IT, Audit).

#### C. Comprehensive Financial Performance Appraisal
The General Secretary performs deep financial efficiency evaluations across all organizational funding streams:
1. **Subvention Budget Vote-Head Execution Rate:**
   - Quarterly release absorption, budget commitment speed, unspent balance risk analysis, and variance tracking.
2. **Non-Tax Revenue (NTR) Target vs Realization:**
   - Detailed revenue appraisal for venue rentals (Arena, Stadium, Tennis, Hostels), licensing fees, and sponsorships against MoFPED annual targets.
3. **National Sports Federation Grant Accountability Compliance:**
   - % of disbursed subventions fully accounted for with valid documentation.
   - Risk categorization of federations (Green = 100% Cleared, Amber = Partial Return, Red = Overdue / High Risk).
4. **Unit Cost & Return on Investment (ROI) Metrics:**
   - Cost-per-athlete funded, cost-per-medal won in international games, maintenance cost per square meter of facility.
5. **Audit Query Resolution Index:**
   - Tracking the remediation speed of Internal Audit and Auditor General queries, minimizing organizational fiscal exposure.

#### Key Asset Appraisal Capabilities:
1. **Ledger Integrity & Value Verification:**
   - Cross-examines accounting entries (`fb_cost`, `adjusted_cost`, `accumulated_depreciation`, `net_book_value`).
   - Verifies depreciation schedules applied under IPSAS 17 (Straight line, 5-20 year useful lives).
2. **Revaluation & Adjustment Authorization:**
   - Formal statutory sign-off on asset revaluations conducted by Chief Government Valuer or licensed valuation surveyors.
3. **Statutory Write-Off & Disposal Approvals:**
   - Formal Accounting Officer approval on Board of Survey recommendations for obsolete computers, unserviceable machinery, and decommissioned vehicles under PPDA asset disposal guidelines.
4. **Impairment Loss Recognition:**
   - Approves impairment adjustments resulting from physical damage, storm impact, or technological obsolescence.

## 4. Database Schema & Technical Architecture

```sql
-- Executive appraisal sessions & determinations by GS
CREATE TABLE executive_appraisals (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    appraisal_code VARCHAR(64) UNIQUE NOT NULL, -- e.g. APPRAISAL-2026-Q4, ASSET-REVAL-2026
    appraisal_type VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- PROPERTY_INVENTORY, STAFF_PERFORMANCE, FINANCIAL_EFFICIENCY, FIXED_ASSETS_LEDGER
    evaluation_period VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL, -- 'FY 2025/2026 Q4'
    evaluated_by UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id), -- General Secretary
    total_portfolio_value_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    overall_performance_score NUMERIC(5,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    executive_findings TEXT,
    accounting_officer_verdict VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'APPROVED', -- APPROVED, APPROVED_WITH_CONDITIONS, REVISED, REJECTED
    statutory_signoff_timestamp TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW(),
    digital_signature_hash VARCHAR(255),
    export_board_package_url TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- Asset valuation sign-offs by GS
CREATE TABLE asset_valuation_signoffs (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    appraisal_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES executive_appraisals(id),
    asset_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES fixed_assets(id),
    recorded_adjusted_cost NUMERIC(18,2) NOT NULL,
    recorded_accumulated_depreciation NUMERIC(18,2) NOT NULL,
    approved_net_book_value NUMERIC(18,2) NOT NULL,
    physical_condition_grade VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL, -- EXCELLENT, GOOD, FAIR, POOR, OBSOLETE
    gs_action VARCHAR(32) NOT NULL DEFAULT 'CONFIRMED_ACTIVE', -- CONFIRMED_ACTIVE, REVALUATION_APPROVED, WRITE_OFF_AUTHORIZED, DISPOSAL_RECOMMENDED
    gs_remarks TEXT,
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);

-- Financial efficiency appraisal scores per department / vote
CREATE TABLE financial_efficiency_appraisals (
    id UUID PRIMARY KEY DEFAULT gen_random_uuid(),
    appraisal_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES executive_appraisals(id),
    department_name VARCHAR(64) NOT NULL,
    allocated_budget_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) NOT NULL,
    actual_expenditure_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) NOT NULL,
    budget_execution_rate NUMERIC(5,2) NOT NULL, -- e.g. 94.5%
    ntr_target_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    ntr_collected_ugx NUMERIC(18,2) DEFAULT 0.00,
    grant_accountability_rate NUMERIC(5,2) DEFAULT 100.00,
    audit_query_count INT DEFAULT 0,
    financial_grade VARCHAR(8) NOT NULL, -- 'A', 'B', 'C', 'D'
    created_at TIMESTAMP WITH TIME ZONE DEFAULT NOW()
);
```
